Reading · The High Line

The High Line is a public park on Manhattan's West Side. It was built on an old elevated railway line that was no longer used. Instead of removing the structure, local residents worked to save it. The first part of the park opened in 2009. Today visitors can walk above the streets, see gardens and public art, and enjoy views of the city and the Hudson River. The High Line has helped change the surrounding neighbourhood and attracts many visitors. However, its popularity has also led to discussions about rising prices in the area. For tourists, it offers a different way of experiencing New York because it combines the city's industrial past with nature and modern design.
